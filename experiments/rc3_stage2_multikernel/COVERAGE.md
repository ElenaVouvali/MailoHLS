# Multi-kernel supervision coverage audit

The audit is generated by `python -m experiments.rc3_stage2_multikernel.audit`.
Its machine-readable receipt is `artifacts/rc3_stage2_multikernel/v1/audit/coverage.json`.

## Authenticated coverage

| Item | Observed coverage |
|---|---:|
| V4 source kernels / families | 40 / 19 |
| Canonical RC3 graphs in the pinned archive | 61 |
| Candidate kernels with an RC3 graph and matching clean-source hash | 40 / 40 |
| Complete action mappings across all source actions | 38 / 40 |
| Train kernels usable after family-preserving filtering | 35 / 37 |
| Training families represented | 17 |
| Validation kernels / disjoint families | 3 / 2 |
| Authenticated train / validation rows in the pilot | 1,164 / 54 |
| Exact device × clock × budget request rows | 18 strata per kernel where covered |

The two excluded train kernels are `rodinia-hotspot-2-pipeline` and
`rodinia-hotspot-3-unroll`: the canonical graph has incomplete compiler action
matching for the registered source actions. No test partition exists in the
authenticated V4 JSONL; test data were not accessed or reassigned.

## What the labels establish

Rows retain complete serialized assignments, objective, source, device,
requested clock, resource budget, latency, and resource-utilization percentages.
They are curated multi-reference SFT examples selected from measured runs. The
four exposures per source/target context often carry distinct full assignments:
492 of 601 train kernel/device/clock/budget contexts contain more than one
assignment. Comparing the lowest-latency assignment among only these curated
exposures gives different assignments across budget strata in 141/187 matched
0.25-vs-0.5 contexts, 151/204 0.5-vs-1.0 contexts, and 134/187 0.25-vs-1.0
contexts. These are sampled-label differences, not proof that the budget binds
in the complete synthesis candidate bank.

The available rows do not carry per-assignment achieved/estimated HLS clock,
exact resource-count receipts, or a complete candidate bank. Utilization is
rounded percentage data. Thus this cohort supports a target-conditioned,
multi-kernel SFT pilot with measured-derived labels; it does not support
timing-qualified winners, exact budget feasibility claims, or physical-QoR
regret. Those outcomes must remain unknown unless joined to authenticated
synthesis receipts for the exact source, full assignment, device, and clock.

The selected V4b encoder is ADP/QoR-trained, not latency-selected. Its Memory C
features are a frozen research input; their use here does not establish that the
encoder is optimal for the latency objective.
