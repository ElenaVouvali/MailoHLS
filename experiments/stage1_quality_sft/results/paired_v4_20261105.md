Neither adapter exactly matches the selected complete configuration on any request. Loop-action accuracy is 0.1323 single-reference and 0.1753 multi-reference; array-action accuracy is 0.0370 and 0.0694. The singleton-only positive-action recall metric is undefined here because no request has a singleton near-optimal candidate set.

The identical-request paired mean difference is +0.0486 action accuracy for multi-reference (20 requests improve, 13 worsen, 21 tie). This is not QoR evidence and has a substantial family split: multi-reference is higher on Viterbi and lower on Pathfinder. Exact complete measured-candidate match and near-optimal measured-pool membership are both zero for both adapters.

The evaluator found 54 paired budget contrasts, 54 clock contrasts and 27 device contrasts per adapter. Single-reference changed predictions in 28/54 budget pairs, 37/54 clock pairs and 12/27 device pairs; multi-reference changed in 10/54, 10/54 and 12/27 respectively. Every pair is classified unsupported for hardware competitiveness because both sides lack exact measured matches. These are descriptive response counts, not evidence that the changes are correct. The 54-case output therefore does not establish that either adapter is hardware-superior.

## Production baseline and diagnostic inference status

The production latency adapter is authenticated as compatible in backbone revision, objective, tokenizer, special-token identities and saved domain registry. Its two 54-case output files were not produced: after the four V4 passes, `nvidia-smi` on the execution host failed with `couldn't communicate with the NVIDIA driver`; the documented Stage-1 Python environment independently reported `torch.cuda.is_available() == False` and `device_count == 0`. The missing production baseline and the requested fresh GAN/Kalman legacy-versus-policy inference are pending. No driver repair, package change, service restart, GPU substitution or process signal was attempted. Historical GAN/Kalman predictions remain immutable.

The source/tool filter is implemented before decoder scoring. Policy V1 was used for the completed source/tool predictions. A separately versioned V2 now models UG1399's `dim=0` all-dimensions semantics: partial partition factors must fit each selected dimension, and complete all-dimension partition uses the product of extents. Regression tests establish that the policy rejects GAN's factor 64 for source extent 50, rejects Kalman's 262,144-element complete partitions under the recorded profile, preserves legal factors 32 and 50, and retains small-array complete partitioning. The V2 decoder has not yet been run because GPU access failed; the completed 54-case policy comparison is specifically V1, and V2 should be evaluated as a distinct follow-up inference pass.

The precise internal XFORM accounting boundary remains subject to the prepared probe.

## Checkpoint decision

**Decision: insufficient evidence; do not promote either V4 adapter and retain the production checkpoint for current production use.** Both V4 adapters generate complete source/tool-admissible outputs, and multi-reference shows a small paired improvement in selected-winner action agreement on these 54 cases, but neither matches any exact measured candidate. V4 HLS comparisons are still running; the compatible production inference baseline and held-out GAN/Kalman constrained re-inference remain unavailable due to the current NVIDIA driver access failure. The present evidence is not sufficient for a fresh Stage-2 initialization decision.

The two V4 adapters preserve the Stage-1 objective-specialized rank-8 LoRA, the common tokenizer/special tokens, source/action marker ordering and directive registry, so their checkpoint contracts are structurally compatible with Stage-2 initialization. Compatibility alone is not a qualification decision. Stage-2 must load the selected frozen Stage-1 adapter and train its own cross-attention; V4 GNN memory is not interchangeable with production V1 cross-attention. Physical device/clock may condition static memory, but budget, objective, gold directives and measured QoR remain outside candidate-independent structural memory.
